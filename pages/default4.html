Frequently Asked Questions
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, completely. There are no long-term lock-ins. If you cancel, you will only be billed for the current active month and your data will be available for export for 30 days.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Absolutely. We offer a 50 percent lifetime discount for verified non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Contact our support team after signing up.
How long does it take to migrate my data?
Our automated importer usually takes less than 10 minutes to bring all your data over from standard CSV files or direct API connections from major platforms.
Is my credit card data safe?
We do not store your credit card data on our servers. All payments are processed securely through our Level 1 PCI compliant payment gateway provider.
